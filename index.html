ABOUT US

Connect. Charge. Go.

Electrosphere is a community EV charging network. We connect electric-vehicle drivers with people who have a charger to share, at homes, apartments and offices, so charging is closer, simpler and fairer for everyone.

1. Connect

Drivers find nearby chargers on the map. Hosts list their charger with plug type, price and time slots.

2. Charge

Book a slot, the host confirms, and you charge after a quick safety check. The host records the start and end battery %.

3. Go

Pay the host directly by scanning their UPI QR. The host approves the payment and you are on your way.

What we believe

Community first: every shared charger makes the network stronger. Clear pricing: you see the rate and the bill before you pay. Safe charging: a safety check before every session.
